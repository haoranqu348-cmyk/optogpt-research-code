## Reading of the evidence

The closest published result is Pfeiffer et al. (2019): a genuinely double-sided B270 sample reaches
1.8% mean total reflectance at 60 deg, but only over 420-680 nm and only after adding a nanoporous
SiO2 layer. Kauppinen et al. independently reports 94% mean transmission at 60 deg for double-sided
graded porous alumina over 350-800 nm. These results make a 2% target physically plausible in a
restricted visible band, while they do not prove that separate s and p means below 2% across
400-1100 nm are achievable with the present dense material database.
